AI for Construction Business Owners

Format 90 minutes · Educational
Prerequisites None

A practical, plain-language introduction to AI for construction business owners and senior leaders. No technical background required. This class is about understanding what AI can realistically do for your business today — and how to evaluate the claims you're hearing from vendors and colleagues.

What you'll leave with

  • A clear mental model for what AI is and isn't good at in construction contexts
  • A framework for identifying which operational problems in your business are good candidates for AI
  • Practical questions to ask when evaluating AI tools and vendors
  • Confidence to have informed conversations about AI with your team and vendors

A practical, hands-on session where you build and test an AI assistant tailored to a real use case in your construction business.   • Skills to continue building and refining on your own after the session
  • A reference guide covering what you built and how to extend it
