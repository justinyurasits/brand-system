Consulting

Work with me

I work directly with enterprises and construction businesses on AI strategy, product design, and implementation. Two tracks, equal weight — choose the one that fits your context, or describe a problem and we'll figure out the right approach together.

Consulting paths

Track one

Enterprise AI

Strategic and technical AI consulting for enterprise organizations building or maturing AI-powered products and systems — with particular depth in conversational AI, agentic systems, and the product challenges of deploying AI in complex organizations.

Problems I work on

  • Moving from AI experimentation to deliberate AI product strategy
  • Designing conversational and agentic systems with reliable, testable behavior
  • Building evaluation frameworks that make product quality legible
  • Navigating the tradeoffs between AI capability and operational predictability
  • Aligning engineering, product, and business stakeholders around realistic AI outcomes
  • Reviewing and improving existing AI systems and architectures

What working together looks like

  • Fractional AI product leadership and strategic guidance
  • System and architecture review with actionable recommendations
  • Hands-on design and implementation work for specific system components
  • Team enablement and decision-support through a build

Engagements vary in scope and structure. Contract, C2C, and fractional arrangements are available. No published rates — the right structure depends on the problem.

Track two

AI for Construction Businesses

Applied AI consulting for construction companies, owners, and teams — grounded in direct construction industry experience. The focus is on operational problems where AI can produce measurable, practical value, not on technology for its own sake.

Problems I work on

  • Identifying which operational problems in your business are genuinely worth solving with AI
  • Designing AI systems around how construction information actually flows — not how software vendors say it does
  • Implementing Construction OS or similar operational AI approaches in a specific project context
  • Document AI for RFIs, submittals, specs, and field communication
  • Evaluating AI vendors and tools against your actual operational needs
  • Building internal AI capabilities without depending on a single vendor

What working together looks like

  • Problem assessment and AI opportunity identification
  • System design and implementation for specific operational workflows
  • Construction OS implementation and customization
  • Education and enablement for construction teams adopting AI tools
